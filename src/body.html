      Questions buyers ask.

      What is the minimum order?

      Orders start from 50 cartons and go up to full 20ft and 40ft containers. The catalogue lists carton and container figures for every line.

      Can you pack under our brand?

      Yes. PURE offers end-to-end private label manufacturing: product development, packaging and branding, from concept to final product.

      Which sectors do you supply?

      Retail, hotels, restaurants and cafés, corporate, industrial and bulk supply, in local and international markets.

      How is quality controlled?

      Agricultural engineers monitor crops against Maximum Residue Limits, field records are checked before raw materials reach the factory, and products are regularly tested by certified laboratories for heavy metals and pesticide residues.

      Do you handle shipping and export?

      Yes. PURE provides import and export, logistics coordination and regulatory compliance, with a logistics team that delivers to any part of the world.

      Which pack sizes are available?

      From 125 g sardine tins to 3 kg catering tins, 400 g frozen bags, 2.5 kg fries, 1 kg oils and pulses up to 5 kg. Each line's sizes are in the catalogue.
